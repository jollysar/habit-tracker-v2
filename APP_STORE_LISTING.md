- **Bundle identifier:** `com.jollysar.habitree`
- **Developer/author:** jollysar
- **App Store seller:** Apple displays the legal name attached to the developer account for an individual, or the registered entity name for an organisation.
- **Primary category:** Productivity
- **Secondary category:** Health & Fitness
- **Age rating:** Complete Apple’s questionnaire accurately; the current app content is expected to qualify for 4+.
- **Copyright:** Enter `2026` followed by the legal person or entity that owns the app; do not use a brand name unless it is the legal rights holder.

> Name clearance remains a release gate. Another habit-tracking app already uses “Habitree” on the App Store. Adding “Grow” helps the listing but does not remove trademark or App Review risk.

## Listing copy

**Subtitle**

Grow your habits daily

**Promotional text**

Build routines that feel alive. Complete daily and weekly habits, grow your plants, and turn consistency into a garden—all with private, local-first tracking.

**Description**

Habitree makes consistency visible.

Create daily habits and flexible weekly goals, then watch each streak grow from a seed into a tree. The calm, focused design keeps today’s priorities clear without turning self-improvement into another source of noise.

• Track daily check-offs and weekly numeric goals
• Grow a plant as each streak develops
• Review your week, calendar, and long-term patterns
• Schedule thoughtful on-device reminders
• Choose an accent colour, appearance, and first day of the week
• Back up and restore your data when you choose
• Keep your habit data on your device—no account, advertising, or behavioural tracking

Habitree is a general productivity tool and does not provide medical advice.

## App Privacy answers

For the current release, select **Data Not Collected** only after confirming the submitted archive contains no additional SDK or network feature. Habit data stays on-device. A support email is initiated by the user in their mail app and is described in the privacy policy.

## Export compliance

The project declares that it does not use non-exempt encryption. Reconfirm this answer whenever networking, authentication, encryption, or another SDK is added.

## Review notes

Habitree works without an account. All sample and user-created habit data is local. Notification permission is requested only when the user enables reminders. Backup and restore are user-initiated from Settings. Include concise steps for any feature App Review might not find immediately.
