Habitree support

Need a hand? Try the quick fixes below or email us. Habitree has no online account, so include only the information you are comfortable sharing.

Email support

Reminders are not appearing

  1. Open Habitree Settings and confirm reminders are enabled for the habit.
  2. Open your device Settings, choose Notifications, then Habitree, and allow notifications.
  3. Check Focus modes, quiet hours, and Scheduled Summary settings.

My habits are not on another device

Habitree is local-first and does not currently sync between devices. Use the backup/export tools in Settings to make a copy before changing or resetting a device.

Back up before troubleshooting

In Habitree Settings, create a backup and save it somewhere you can find again. Keep that file private: it can contain habit names, schedules, and completion history.

Delete your data

Delete habits from their habit menu. To remove the app’s remaining local data, delete Habitree from your device. Files you previously exported must be deleted separately from their saved location.

Accessibility feedback

Habitree supports keyboard navigation, system appearance, reduced-motion preferences, and accessible labels. If something prevents you from using the app, email the device model, operating-system version, and the screen where the issue occurs.

When contacting support

Please include the Habitree version, device model, operating-system version, what you expected, and what happened. Do not send a backup file unless support specifically asks for it and you are comfortable sharing its contents.

Important note

Habitree is a general productivity tool. It is not medical advice, a medical device, or an emergency service.
