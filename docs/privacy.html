Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026

Habitree: Grow is operated by jollysar. The app is designed to work without an account, advertising, analytics, or behavioural tracking.

Information stored on your device

Habitree stores the information you enter—including habit names, schedules, goals, completion history, reminder preferences, appearance settings, and app preferences—in the app’s local storage on your device. This information is used only to provide the app’s features.

Information we collect

Habitree does not transmit your habit data to jollysar and does not operate a server that collects it. The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or an account system.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notification schedules and delivery are handled on your device through the operating system. You can change notification permission in your device settings at any time.

Backups, exports, and deletion

Any backup or export is created only when you request it and is saved to a location you choose. You are responsible for files you export or share. You can delete individual habits within the app. Uninstalling Habitree normally removes data in its app container, but copies in device backups or files you exported remain under your control.

Feedback and support

The feedback option opens your email app. If you choose to send a message, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you provide. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot, and maintain the app, and retain it only as reasonably necessary for those purposes or legal obligations. You can ask us to delete a support conversation.

Platform services

Apple may process information relating to your App Store download, purchases, device, diagnostics, or use of Apple services under Apple’s own privacy policy. That processing is controlled by Apple and is separate from Habitree’s local app data.

Children’s privacy

Habitree is a general-audience productivity app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no account is required and habit data remains local, we do not receive a user’s age or habit content.

Security

Habitree limits app permissions and keeps its content local. No method of storage is completely risk-free, so keep your device protected with operating-system updates, a passcode, and secure backups.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised, and material changes will be communicated through the app or its store listing where appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests relating to support correspondence, contact sargaur03@gmail.com.
