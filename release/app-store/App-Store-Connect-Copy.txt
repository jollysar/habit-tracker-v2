Promotional text
Build routines that feel alive. Complete daily and weekly habits, grow your plants, and turn consistency into a garden—all with private, local-first tracking.

Description
Habitree makes consistency visible.

Create daily habits and flexible weekly goals, then watch each streak grow from a seed into a tree. The calm, focused design keeps today’s priorities clear without turning self-improvement into another source of noise.

• Track daily check-offs and weekly goals
• Grow a plant as each streak develops
• Review your week, calendar, monthly progress, and long-term patterns
• Schedule thoughtful on-device reminders
• Choose your appearance, accent colour, and first day of the week
• Back up and restore your data when you choose
• Keep your habit data on your device—no account, advertising, or behavioural tracking

Habitree is a general productivity tool and does not provide medical advice.
